## Record

The governance release, hostile-tested: the review trail now records who
approved what and where the evidence sits; the entire
measure→miss→repair→prove lifecycle runs over plain HTTP; a 1,120-chunk
adversarial corpus exposed systematic restraint leakage, the repair loop
measurably disclosed its own limits, and the resulting grounding
semantics (author-scoped sentence gates + direction-faithful template
triggers) closed it to 0/13 violations at full recall. All dependency
PRs processed. 351 tests.

- **Dependency PRs processed (renovate #15/#16/#24/#26/#27/#29,
  actions #28):** lru 0.18, tokenizers 0.23 (onnx feature
  check-verified; note: the `paste` advisory persists via
  macro_rules_attribute — earlier prediction corrected), tower-http 0.7
  (reqwest's transitive 0.6 copy stays, following reqwest), sha2 0.11 +
  hmac 0.13 together (digest-0.11 family: KeyInit import + manual hex
  encoding in auth; SHA-256/HMAC outputs unchanged, stored hashes and
  sessions stay valid), mlua 0.12 (sandbox + instruction-limit tests
  green), actions/checkout v7. Full gates on the combined tree.
- **Grounding gates + template triggers (D1-D4,
  decision_grounding_gates.md):** the design outcome of the hostile
  eval, every option simulated before deciding
  (`examples/endpoint_gate_sim.rs` — global gates rejected BY
  MEASUREMENT: they cost up to 14/15 recall on honest kits). Landed:
  per-rule opt-in `require_in_sentence` (sentence-scoped endpoint gate,
  enum-typed so typos fail loudly, per-occurrence like negation),
  `{source}`/`{target}` template triggers (direction-faithful matching,
  expansion recorded verbatim with span), absence claims verified
  already-handled by negation awareness (no code), and distinct-fact
  eval counting (hostile "14/18" was 9/13 distinct; baselines
  re-measured, results files annotated, conclusions unchanged). Neurons
  still cannot touch matching semantics; vetoes stay plain-presence;
  default behavior byte-identical without opt-ins.
- **Blocker-repair experiment on the hostile corpus (2026-07-06):** the
  measured answer to "can governed blockers close systematic leakage?"
  — no, and the governance said so itself. New runner
  `examples/blocker_repair.rs` (restraint mirror of blind_eval): 9/9
  violation-directed blockers proposed, recall held 37/37, but only
  **1 of 9 distinct violations closed** — the one whose coverage
  simulation showed zero uncovered chunks. The other 8 suppressed ~44
  of ~45 violating chunks each and were flagged `reviewer: incomplete`
  AT PROPOSAL TIME; one uncovered chunk keeps a fact grounded.
  Conclusion recorded in the dossier (evidence item 10): chunk-local
  vetoes repair occasional illegitimate context, not systematic
  cross-company/direction leakage — that needs grounding-semantics
  design work (endpoint-presence, direction-aware triggers), now
  motivated by numbers.
- **Full-suite verification sweep #3 (2026-07-06, post quick-wins arc):**
  all gates and all four benchmark harnesses re-run — 349/349 tests,
  clippy/fmt/CI clean, every figure at its recorded baseline. Notables:
  grounding WITH QW5's span provenance measured 5.8/6.3/17.1 µs per
  chunk at 0/8/64 vetoes — dead center of the pre-span band, confirming
  the provenance computation free at bench resolution on a second
  independent run; in-memory CGQL @ c=128 back at 132.2k req/s (the
  prior sweep's ambient-contention episode fully cleared); persistent
  reads still indistinguishable from in-memory (139.8k point reads/s).
- **QW8 paper cuts (quick-wins-2026-07.md):** the blind-kit TEMPLATE
  gains the measured meta-question authoring note (phrase
  absence-reasoning ground truth as assertable facts), and DataOps guide
  03 ships the measured-good `graph_facts` answer prompt (exhaustive
  selection + verbatim copying — the wording worth 47%→78% answer recall
  on the blind kits, with fabrication symbolically checkable).
- **QW5 — trigger span provenance (quick-wins-2026-07.md):** grounded
  fact edges now record `trigger_start`/`trigger_end`, the byte range of
  the affirmed trigger occurrence in the evidence chunk's original text
  — "look, right here" instead of "trust me". Casefolded match offsets
  are mapped back through folding expansions (non-ASCII test-pinned),
  and when a negated occurrence precedes the licensing one the span
  points at the licensing occurrence. Additive; grounding benchmark
  unchanged.
- **QW4 — CLI review workflow (quick-wins-2026-07.md):**
  `cognigraph neuron pending [SPACE]` digests the review queue (oldest
  first: fact, triggers, author, rationale), and `cognigraph neuron
  show KEY` prints the neuron together with every chunk its triggers
  match — full evidence text inline — plus any trigger matching no
  chunk, the dead-trigger signal to question before accepting. Read
  path only; no server changes.
- **QW3 — repair over HTTP: `POST /construct/propose`
  (quick-wins-2026-07.md):** gap-directed neuron proposing as a route —
  gaps passed explicitly or measured server-side from the stored eval
  spec, evidence retrieved via the backend's BM25, verbatim-self-checked
  proposals stored as `proposed` with authorship (id conflicts and
  validation failures become visible skips). The server gains a
  completion provider (`OPENAI_API_KEY`/`GEMINI_API_KEY`,
  `COGNIGRAPH_COMPLETION_MODEL`). With ingest (v2.1.0) and evaluate
  (QW2), the whole measure→miss→repair→prove lifecycle now runs over
  plain HTTP — verified live end to end with curl only.
- **QW2 — measurement over HTTP: `POST /construct/evaluate`
  (quick-wins-2026-07.md):** recall + restraint + missing/violating
  facts against the live graph, from an inline eval spec or one stored
  in the `eval_specs` collection (keyed by space id). Deterministic, no
  model; read scope despite the POST (separate merged router), so
  periodic degradation checks are a curl in a cron job.
- **QW1 — reviewer attribution on the neuron lifecycle
  (quick-wins-2026-07.md):** every neuron now records `proposed_by`/
  `proposed_at` at creation and `reviewed_by`/`reviewed_at` (plus an
  optional `review_note`) on every accept/reject/retire — the
  authenticated user, or `"anonymous"` when auth is disabled, so the
  audit trail is honest about the mode it was written under. Attribution
  shows in `GET /neurons` and on graduation candidates; CLI transitions
  take `--note`. A transition without attribution is no longer possible.
