## Record

The measured-authority release: who and what may hold authority in the
construction loop is now decided by measurement and bound by attested
config. The head-to-head the thesis needed: graph substrate beats
plain vector RAG 2.4× on answer recall with restraint 0/54 vs 4/54
under the identical answering technique. The model matrix measured
three models across four roles — the incumbent judge stays qualified
because its errors are calibrated below the authority line (Gemini and
gpt-5.4 both disqualified at/above it; replication vetoed gpt-5.4's
lucky 0/76 first run) — and Lane A became model-bound
(`qualified_judges`). The agreement lane (Lane A+) then converted the
measured disjoint blind spots into new autonomy: two-judge concordance
auto-accepts the direction-critical class, attested per pair by the
offline concordance instrument (mini+gpt-5.4: zero concordant
false-accepts worst-case across four runs). The answer layer closed
its last diagnosed residue (evidence-augmented trace: procurement
15/17 best-ever, restraint 0/54 with raw prose — substrate symmetry
measured in both directions), and the loop is pilot-ready: resumable
review drains with per-call metrics, queue pagination, and a 10k
dry-run showing an order of magnitude of headroom on every
deterministic surface. Gemini keeps full measurement support (native
responseJsonSchema, 0 retries) and zero authority. 374 tests; all
benchmarks at baseline.

- **Pilot-readiness hardening.** `/construct/review` gains `limit`
  (judge at most N proposals per call, oldest key first, deterministic)
  and per-call operational metrics (`reviewed`, `pending_remaining`,
  `judge_calls`, `elapsed_ms`); the drain is resumable because review
  now judges UNJUDGED proposals only — a judged-and-queued item awaits
  a human, and re-judging it would stall a limited drain on the same
  queue head (`rejudge: true` overrides after a POLICY_REV change).
  `GET /neurons` gains `limit`/`offset` pagination; CLI `neuron pending
  [--limit N]`. New instrument `examples/pilot_dryrun.rs` (deterministic,
  no LLM): at 10k chunks / 2k proposals — ingest 175 ms (57 chunks/ms,
  4,500 fact edges), idempotent re-ingest 137 ms, evaluate 30/30
  recall + 0/30 restraint (gates + negation holding at scale) in <1 ms,
  proposal queue stored in 5 ms and paginated in 2 ms. The loop's
  deterministic surfaces carry an order of magnitude of headroom; the
  pilot's real unknowns are LLM-bound, exactly where the slice/drain
  mechanics now apply. Drain semantics test-pinned (25 proposals in
  three limited slices, then rejudge). 374 tests.
- **The agreement lane (Lane A+)** — design session D1–D6,
  decision_agreement_lane.md. Two judges with measured-disjoint blind
  spots (the incumbent catches negation, its partner catches
  direction) may jointly auto-accept the direction-critical class Lane
  A excludes: hints on templated/gated EXISTING rules. Concordance
  semantics: both judges run the full two-stage pipeline; auto-accept
  only when BOTH accept at/above the agreement threshold; any
  disagreement queues with both verdicts attached; partner missing or
  pair mismatch degrades to queue (`lane_a_plus: withheld`) — never to
  a single judge. Policy schema: `auto_accept.agreement { kinds,
  judges (exactly two, the PAIR is the attested unit), min_confidence,
  concordance_measured }`, refused without the attestation. The
  attestation instrument is `examples/concordance_sim.rs`
  (deterministic, offline, over preserved replay verdicts): the
  shipped default pairing mini+gpt-5.4 measures **0 concordant
  false-accepts at threshold over 74 shared cases, worst-case across
  four runs** (43/65 good hints concordantly accepted); mini+gemini
  also 0 (30/65), kept as the documented cross-family alternative.
  A+ acceptances stamp `reviewed_by: judges:<a>+<b>@POLICY_REV` with
  both verdicts stored; audit sampling applies;
  `COGNIGRAPH_JUDGE_PARTNER_MODEL` wires the partner. New-triple hints
  and blockers stay out; all boundaries kept. Tests pin
  concordance-accept with pair attribution, disagreement-queue with
  both verdicts, pair-mismatch withholding, and attestation refusal.
  373 tests.
