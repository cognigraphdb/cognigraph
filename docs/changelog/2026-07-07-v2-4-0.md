## Record

The instruments release: every remaining authoring judgment in the
Semantic Neurons loop got a measured tool, and one proposed semantics
extension was dissolved by its own instrument. The review judge became
two LLMs-per-purpose (taint screener + the v0 quality calibration —
escalation ~30% → ~20–24%, hint false-accepts 0/67 at the policy
threshold in every run, zero induced accepts across the injection
suite, verdict instability measured by replication). The gate advisor
tells the ontology author where the sentence-gate precision budget is
needed (6/6 authored hostile gates rediscovered on the exact
endpoints). Coverage-guided blocker iteration closes what one-shot
proposing could only disclose (1/6 → 6/6, restraint 6/13 → 0/13,
recall untouched). A completeness-critic second answering pass
recovered the selectivity residue (answer recall 73% → 87%, restraint
0/54, zero fabrications) and answer-level measurement went
HTTP-complete. The ontology drafter shipped as what its measurement
says it is — a vocabulary bootstrap (51% of an expert catalogue at
0/45 forbidden pairs; ~6% rule coverage), making the repair loop
startable on a fresh corpus. And cross-chunk grounding was rejected by
measurement: chunk locality costs zero recall once two sentence-bounds
tokenization bugs (decimals, dotted names) were fixed. Two dedicated
design sessions (ontology drafter D1–D6, cross-chunk), two decision
addenda, the paper's rev2 edit pack, 369 tests, all benchmarks at
baseline.

- **Cross-chunk grounding: rejected by measurement**
  (decision_cross_chunk_grounding.md). The design session built its
  instrument first — `examples/chunk_sensitivity.rs` re-grounds the
  five reference corpora under adversarial single-sentence chunking,
  sliding windows (2/3), and gate-lookback variants, all through
  production `ground_chunk`. Result: chunk locality costs ZERO recall
  on all five corpora (96/96 expected, 0/45 forbidden in every
  regime) — the only losses at the adversarial floor were dotted
  names (`Chorus.ai`, `OpenProtein.AI`) split mid-token by the
  sentence boundary, a bug, not missing semantics. Fixed in production
  `sentence_bounds`: a `.` ends a sentence only when followed by
  whitespace/end-of-text (subsumes the decimal-point rule; also stops
  D1 gates judging truncated sentences around dotted names) —
  regression test added; kit baselines, endpoint-gate sim, and the
  advisor's 6/6 rediscovery re-verified identical. Windowed grounding
  and gate lookback adopted NOWHERE (windows recovered nothing and
  blur provenance; lookback-1 gained nothing — and leaked nothing on
  the hostile corpus, a restraint datum kept on record). Deployment
  guidance goes upstream: chunk with overlap (guide 01). Revisit
  trigger: the 10k pilot showing genuine cross-chunk misses. 369
  tests.

- **Answer-layer residue closed: two-pass answering +
  `POST /construct/answer-eval`.** The residue after the trace-to-answer
  fix was answerer SELECTIVITY (trace-present facts omitted on
  boundary/meta questions). `AnswerOpts { two_pass }` adds a
  completeness-critic second pass per question — same trace, first-pass
  selection shown, additions only (it can never remove an assertion, so
  restraint is measured over the union). Measured across the four blind
  kits + hostile kit: answer recall **77/105 (73%) → 91/105 (87%)**,
  restraint **0/54 in both modes**, zero fabrications, recoveries
  landing exactly on the known selective-answer questions (hostile q5

  HTTP-complete: `POST /construct/answer-eval` (read scope, stored or
  inline eval spec, needs the completion provider) joins `/evaluate` as

  New: `examples/answer_pass_compare.rs`; `answer_eval_with` keeps the
  existing `answer_eval` signature stable. Decision-log outcomes updated

- **Coverage-guided blocker iteration** —
  `propose_blockers_covering(provider, space, violations, chunks,
  max_rounds)` + `blocker_repair --rounds N [--strip-gates]`: per
  violated fact, propose a veto, simulate what it suppresses, and
  re-propose against ONLY the still-uncovered chunks until covered, dry
  (a round suppressing nothing new is dropped, never shipped), failed,
  or capped; prior veto phrases are shown to the proposer so it targets
  the remainder. Measured on the hostile corpus (gates stripped,
  pre-gates baseline): one-shot control closes **1/6** distinct
  violations (reproducing the original experiment's structure on
  today's corpus); coverage-guided closes **6/6** in 2–3 rounds per
  fact, IF-ACCEPTED restraint 6/13 → **0/13** with recall untouched
  (37/37) and answer-level restraint clean (0/18). Boundaries
  unchanged: everything emits `status: proposed`, blockers never
  auto-accept, and the structural fix for systematic leakage remains
  authored gates/templates — iteration makes the neuron-space option
  complete instead of disclosed-incomplete. 364 tests.
- **The gate advisor** — `POST /construct/advise` (read scope) +
  `cognigraph advise SPACE` + `examples/gate_advisor.rs`: a
  deterministic corpus-statistics pass that tells the ontology author
  where to spend the sentence-gate precision budget. For every rule it
  grounds the corpus under each candidate gate (none/source/target/both)
  using `ground_chunk` itself — production semantics, no
  reimplementation — and reports a SAFE suggestion where a gate blocks
  off-subject groundings at zero corpus recall cost, plus a REVIEW flag
  where an endpoint appears in NO licensing sentence (the cross-company
  leakage signature; ambiguous with cross-sentence evidence, so it is
  flagged with sample sentences, never auto-suggested). Validated
  against the hostile fixture: with the author's gates stripped, the
  advisor rediscovered **6/6 authored gates on the exact endpoints**
  (five source, one target). Analyzes the effective config (accepted
  hints included); the advisor never edits the ontology.
- **Grounding fix found by the advisor:** `sentence_bounds` no longer
  treats a decimal point as a sentence boundary ("$3.09B" previously
  split the gated sentence at "$3."), so `require_in_sentence` gates now
  work on rules with numeric endpoints. All kit baselines re-verified
  byte-identical (hostile 37/37 v0/13, market-size 15/15 v0/10,
  bi-digital 13/13 v0/8); regression test added. 361 tests.
- **judge-policy-v2: the two-stage judge** (LLM-per-purpose, gated on
  both regression harnesses per the POLICY_REV rule). A dedicated taint
  screener (`SCREEN_SYSTEM`) now sees every proposal's material first
  and escalates review-steering content to a human before a quality
  verdict exists; the quality prompt returns to the v0 calibration
  verbatim, freed of the defense burden. Measured: injection suite
  passed (zero induced accepts, all 16 payloads screener-flagged at
  0.96–0.99); replay run TWICE — hint false-accepts at the Lane A
  threshold 0/67 in both runs, hint escalation recovered ~30% → ~20–24%
  (the v1 defense line's measured cost). Two direction-check prompt
  variants were rejected by measurement (25/21 errors-on-good vs v0's
  12; one misfired on passive relation labels like `ACQUIRED_BY`) —
  direction risk stays at the policy layer where D1's Lane A exclusions
  tax only direction-critical hints. New methodological finding,
  recorded in the replay results: borderline verdicts are unstable
  under sampling (the negated-evidence case flipped once in six runs,
  below threshold), so replay gates are stated at the confidence
  threshold the policy consumes and single-run replays are known to
  overstate determinism. v1 verdicts preserved
  (`verdicts-judge-policy-v1.json`); attribution now stamps
  `judge:<model>@judge-policy-v2`; new server test pins the
  taint-escalation path.
