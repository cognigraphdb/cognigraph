# Research ideas

These bookmarks explore ways to extend adaptive retrieval and semantic memory.
They are **not adopted or scheduled commitments**. Each explains the idea, its
relevance and what evidence would justify integration.

- [GraphMAE](graphmae.md): self-supervised graph representation learning.
- [Elastic Weight Consolidation](ewc.md): limiting forgetting in continual learning.

Agent-written pre-implementation plans moved to [the plan archive](../../plans/archive/README.md).
They record intent and sequencing at their dates. Checked boxes and old paths
are historical evidence, not the current product contract; verify delivered
behavior against code, current guides, tests and the relevant decision. Later
implementation may intentionally differ from the proposal.
