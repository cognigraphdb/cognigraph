## Problem

The decision folder contains roughly fifty records spanning standing conventions,
product contracts, research results, amendments, and historical plans. Its README
only explains the naming convention. Readers must reconstruct which decisions
remain current and which were amended by searching the entire folder, contributing
to the contradictory current summaries recorded in CG-23, CG-27, and CG-28.

## Evidence

`docs/decisions/README.md:1-6` has no decision index, status list, or successor map.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Add a maintained index linking each decision, its status, and any successor.
- [x] Distinguish current product contracts, standing conventions, research evidence,
      and superseded history without rewriting historical outcomes.
- [x] Verify all links and identify the current owner document for each major subsystem.

## Resolution

The [decision index](../decisions/README.md) gives every one of the 53 records
a scoped status and successor/amendment explanation, with current owner
documents for 14 areas. Its maintenance procedure and dependency-free
`python3 scripts/check-decision-index.py` check enforce inventory coverage and
resolving local index links/anchors. All historical decision bodies remain
unchanged. The [verification report](decision-index-2026-09-09.md) records the
reconciliation, positive/negative checks, and evidence limits.

See [review evidence](review-2026-09-08.md).
