# CG-176: Artifact custody leaves undetected partial stages after a crash and cannot restore more than one bundle per tenant

- Status: Open
- Priority: P3
- Area: Artifacts / custody and restoration (Enterprise)
- Found: 2026-11-11
- Verification: Pending (source-confirmed in the 2026-11-11 review; not executed)
- Scheduling: The second part needs a contract change; owner decision first.

## Problem and evidence

- **Crash residue.** Restore stages at
  `tenants/.cognigraph-restore-<scope>-<uuid>.partial` and bundle creation at
  `<parent>/.cognigraph-backup-<uuid>.partial`
  (`crates/cognigraph-artifacts/src/recovery.rs:416-462`, `:514-545`).
  `cleanup_stage` is `let _ = fs::remove_dir_all(stage)`. Nothing detects,
  reports or removes a stale stage on a later run. After a kill or power loss
  during a large restore, a full private copy of the tenant's blobs stays
  inside the live content store's `tenants/` directory. The M24 decision says
  a pre-publication failure "removes the owned partial stage", which holds
  only for in-process errors where removal succeeds. A post-publication
  receipt mismatch (`:452-456`) also returns without the "published at"
  wrapper that the other post-publication failures get.
- **One bundle per incarnation.** Plans and bundles are per evidence id, and
  `restore_bundle` requires the tenant scope directory to be absent
  (`recovery.rs:510-512`). `docs/operations/recovery.md:36-39` says the bundle
  and the database backup "must belong to the same recovery plan", but a
  database holds many evidence records. After restoring the newest bundle,
  restoring an older one, needed to re-evaluate or audit it, fails with
  "destination artifact tenant scope already exists". The M24 decision
  documents "never merges"; the operational gap is the finding.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Create and restore detect stale partial stages for the same scope and report or remove them; a cleanup failure is part of the returned error.
- [ ] Either a verified additive restore exists (adds missing blobs, rereads existing ones, never replaces) or a tenant-wide plan covers all retained evidence.
- [ ] The M24 decision and the recovery guide state the crash residue and which evidence a restored scope can serve.
