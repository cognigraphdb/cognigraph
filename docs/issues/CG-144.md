# CG-144: Job records are rewritten whole at every checkpoint and loaded whole at startup

- Status: Open
- Priority: P2
- Area: Server / jobs storage and recovery (Enterprise)
- Found: 2026-11-11
- Verification: Pending (source-confirmed in the 2026-11-11 review; not executed)

## Problem and evidence

A job record carries the submitted `input` (up to 16 MiB) and `_execution`, a
second copy of the chunks, space and neurons
(`crates/cognigraph-server/src/jobs/records.rs`). Each ingest step does at
least three full reads, one payload clone and one full `replace_document`
(`jobs/storage.rs:60-80`, `worker.rs`). A 10 MiB input at batch size 25 is
about 400 steps of roughly 20 MiB each.

Recovery and the idle-fence check call `list_raw`, which is
`list_documents(JOBS, None, None)`: every hot record, in full, in one vector
(`jobs/recovery.rs:59`, `tenant_lifecycle.rs:121`). Reconciliation pages live
and archived records with all fields while holding the process-wide
transition lock. Terminal jobs stay hot until archived by hand
([CG-145](CG-145.md)) and the archive is never purged. The M17 decision
promises "multiple bounded slices", and the rationale in `jobs/mod.rs:4-7`
("one document replacement on every backend") predates the Native-only
decision.

Expected consequence, by arithmetic rather than measurement: after a few
hundred large ingest jobs, startup recovery materializes tens of gigabytes and
stalls or runs out of memory before HTTP binds, on every restart. Snapshot
import and tenant activation use the same path.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Checkpoints write only mutable state; the frozen input is stored once and not re-read per step.
- [ ] Recovery, the idle-fence check and reconciliation use projections or byte-bounded pages, and only non-terminal jobs load their payload.
- [ ] A test or benchmark recovers a store holding many large terminal jobs within a stated memory bound.
