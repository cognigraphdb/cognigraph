## Problem and evidence

The two largest modules in `cognigraph-construct` are about three times the
300–400 line recommendation (a guide, not a cap), and both still hold their tests inline, against the
rule that tests live in separate files:

| File | Lines | Inline tests | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| `crates/cognigraph-construct/src/draft.rs` | 1,197 | from line 693 (~500 lines) | Prompts and schemas (`ENTITY_SYSTEM`, `RULE_SYSTEM`, `entity_schema`, `rule_schema`, lines 26–97), sampling and the two drafters (`draft_space_type`, `draft_space_type_per_document`, 111–366), and the finalisation pass (`merge_drafted`, `finalize_draft`, `ADMINISTRATIVE_ENTITY_TYPES`, `drop_administrative_relations`, `canonicalize_entity_identity`, `drop_non_specific_triggers`, `merge_entities`, `merge_rules`, 386–692). |
| `crates/cognigraph-construct/src/grounding.rs` | 1,190 | from line 804 (~390 lines) | Negation and clause logic (`NEGATION_CUES`, `affirms_phrase*`, `map_cf_offset`, `clause_negation_index`, `token_is_negation`, lines 13–123 and 475–513), sentence boundaries (`NON_TERMINAL_ABBREVIATIONS`, `word_before_dot`, `is_sentence_boundary`, `sentence_ranges_cf`, 124–185 and 323–338), the advisory semantics detector (`SEMANTICS_REV`, `RELATION_STOPWORDS`, `stem`, `entity_surfaces`, `names_entity`, `merely_co_listed`, `relation_vocabulary_absent`, `relation_semantics_signals`, 186–322), trigger expansion and the sentence gate (`EntitySurfaces`, `indexed_entity_surfaces`, `find_expanded_trigger`, `substitute_authored_placeholders`, `sentence_gate_accepts`, 339–474), and the public grounding contract (`effective_config`, `VetoRule`, `effective_vetoes`, `mentions`, `GroundedFact`, `ground_chunk`, 514–803). |

`grounding.rs` is the soundness core of Semantic Neurons (negation
awareness, sentence-scoped subject binding), so its split must be
behaviour-neutral and the existing tests are the guard.

## Progress

2026-09-22: the inline tests of both files moved to `draft_tests.rs` and
`grounding_tests.rs` with identical test lists
([v2.7.23](../changelog/2026-09-22-construct-tests-separate-files.md)).
`draft.rs` is now 692 lines and `grounding.rs` 803. The remaining split is
open and is a cohesion judgment: the owner's guidance is that 300–400 lines
is recommended, not required.

## Proposed split

- `draft.rs` keeps `DRAFT_REV`, `DraftReport` and the public drafter entry
  points; `draft/prompts.rs` takes the two system prompts and schemas;
  `draft/finalize.rs` takes the finalisation pass with
  `ADMINISTRATIVE_ENTITY_TYPES`. Inline tests move to `draft_tests.rs` (or
  one `_tests.rs` per new module) via `#[path]`.
- `grounding.rs` keeps the public contract (`effective_config`,
  `effective_vetoes`, `mentions`, `GroundedFact`, `ground_chunk`,
  `affirms_phrase`, `sentence_bounds`); `grounding/negation.rs` takes the
  cue list, clause index and case-folded offset mapping;
  `grounding/sentences.rs` takes boundary detection and abbreviations;
  `grounding/semantics.rs` takes the advisory detector under
  `SEMANTICS_REV`; `grounding/triggers.rs` takes placeholder expansion and
  the sentence gate. Inline tests move to sibling `_tests.rs` files.
- Public re-exports keep `cognigraph_construct::grounding::{affirms_phrase,
  sentence_bounds, GroundedFact, ground_chunk, effective_config,
  effective_vetoes, relation_semantics_signals, SEMANTICS_REV}` and
  `cognigraph_construct::draft::*` stable for `directed.rs`, `ingest.rs`,
  `advisor.rs`, the server routes and the examples.

## Resolution

Resolved 2026-09-23 in v2.7.36 under the
[module split decision](../decisions/decision_construct_module_splits.md), along
the proposed split. The "identical evaluation results" criterion is enforced
by a new snapshot test over the four public kits
(`crates/cognigraph-construct/tests/grounding_snapshot.rs`): recorded before the
split, unchanged after it, and shown to fail when grounding behavior changes.
The grounding contract is unchanged. Refactor only; no behavior change.
