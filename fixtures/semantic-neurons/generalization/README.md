# Generalization fixture source and attribution

The text excerpts in `crowdstrike_2024.chunks.jsonl` come from Wikipedia
contributors' [2024 CrowdStrike-related IT outages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_CrowdStrike-related_IT_outages).
The article credits its contributors through its revision history and provides
text under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Those terms apply to the retained excerpts and their textual adaptations here;
the repository's software license does not replace them.

The research preparation split excerpts into JSONL chunks and added local ids
and titles. `crowdstrike_2024.chunks.v2.jsonl` contains authored paraphrases for
the document-revision experiment; neuron records retain selected evidence
excerpts. These transformations are not new source reporting or endorsements.
The exact upstream revision was not recorded by the historical preparation;
the files remain frozen, and the current article is not claimed to be that
original revision. See the [experiment record](../../../docs/research/semantic-neurons/generalization.md)
and [revision experiment](../../../docs/research/semantic-neurons/self-healing.md).
